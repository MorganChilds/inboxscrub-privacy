InboxScrub Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

InboxScrub ("we," "us," "the app") is an email cleanup application that connects to your Gmail or Outlook account to help you search, organize, and delete unwanted email through natural-language chat commands. This policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and how it's protected.

What We Access and Why

When you connect your Gmail or Outlook account, InboxScrub requests permission to:

  • Read, modify, and organize your email (move to trash, archive, mark as read, move into folders, create filters) - this is the core function of the app, used only in response to your explicit commands.
  • Manage mailbox settings (e.g., creating filters for the "Auto Clean" and "Pause" features) - used only for features you explicitly enable.

We do not read the content of your emails for any purpose other than fulfilling the specific action you request. We do not scan, analyze, or use your email content for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the cleanup action you asked for.

Data We Store

  • Refresh tokens (only if you enable Auto Clean): a securely stored credential that allows scheduled cleanup rules to run automatically, even when the app is closed.
  • Chat history: your conversations with the app are saved so you can revisit past chats. You can delete individual conversations at any time.
  • Unsubscribe, block, and pause records: a list of senders you've chosen to manage.
  • Usage counts: for free-tier users, we track the number of actions taken per month to enforce the free usage limit.
  • Subscription/billing status: if you purchase InboxScrub Pro, we store your subscription status. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple or Google - we never see your payment card details.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or email content with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform: hosts our backend, database, and authentication.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): processes the text of your chat commands to determine what action to take. Only your command text is sent - not your email content.
  • Google Sign-In / Gmail API and Microsoft Graph API: used to authenticate and act on your connected account.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: process subscription payments; we never receive your payment details directly.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can disconnect your account at any time by signing out. To request permanent deletion of your stored data, contact us below.

Children's Privacy

InboxScrub is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
