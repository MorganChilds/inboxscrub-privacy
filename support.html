Common Questions

How do I connect my Gmail or Outlook account?
Open InboxScrub and tap "Connect Gmail" or "Connect Outlook" on the sign-in screen. You'll be taken through your provider's normal sign-in process.

Is my email data safe?
InboxScrub only acts on your email in direct response to commands you give it. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone to manage or cancel anytime.

Something isn't working - what do I do?
Email us at the address above with a description of the issue, and we'll help you sort it out.
